# Overlays

Modals keep users in context without navigating to a new page. Size determines which modal to use: simple actions use a standard modal; complex or long content uses a large one.

## Rules

1. Use an overlay when a task is secondary to what's behind it. Navigate to a page when the content is a destination in its own right.
2. Use a standard modal for confirmations, short forms, and single focused actions.
3. Use a large modal (80% of the viewport) for complex or long-scrolling content — multi-step forms, detail views, and records with multiple sections.
4. Never nest a modal inside another modal.
5. Close with a dismiss action or backdrop click.

A confirmation dialog is a modal — see [Confirmations](/design/patterns/confirmations) for its content and button rules.
