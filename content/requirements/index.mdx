# Requirement Analysis

> Standards for documenting requirements.

The journey starts when stakeholders have an idea for a product, a feature, or a change. They communicate that idea to the PM(Product Manager) and the PM understands the overall concept. But the concept is still in their heads or in some hard-to-decipher drawings on the whiteboard or their notebook. 

Now we need to put the concept into a form that the engineering team will be able to understand, digest, and respond to. This is where we say “Okay, this is where we write the requirements.”

There are 6 ingredients that go into writing requirements:
- 4 of them are mandatory, and 2 are optional.

1. **Problem Overview**
2. **Solution** — short summary *(optional)* with high-level diagram
3. **Stories** — stories in flow order
   - 3.1 Story
   - 3.2 Diagrams / wireframe sketches *(optional)*
   - 3.3 Acceptance criteria (with field validations; story-specific out-of-scope here too)
4. **Out of scope** — solution-level *(optional)*
5. **Open questions** *(optional)*
6. **Timeline**
