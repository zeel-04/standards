# Writing

Three guiding principles, plus a short list of supporting rules. Every principle has at least one before/after example.

## Principles

1. Simple and easy to understand
2. Short and sweet
3. No redundancy

## Supporting rules

A handful of habits that follow from the three principles above:

- **Use tables for repetitive structured data.** Field validations, role/permission matrices, status mappings — anything with a fixed set of columns repeated across rows reads faster as a table than as prose. Reserve prose for narrative that doesn't fit a grid.

- **Be consistent with terminology and formatting.**

- **One responsibility per section.** Each section should have one thing a reviewer can accept or reject.
